# Unit Economics

If Credex deployed this tool tomorrow, the core objective is to use the AI Spend Audit as a high-value, low-friction lead magnet. The ultimate goal is converting these leads into booked consultations and, subsequently, lucrative credit or spend-management contracts.


- **Average Spend Volume:** A typical Series A startup spends roughly $1M annually on SaaS, Cloud, and AI.
- **Take Rate/Interchange:** Assuming a standard ~1.5% net revenue margin on spend volume.
- **Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR):** $1M * 1.5% = $15,000 ARR per converted startup.
- **LTV:** Assuming a 3-year lifespan, the Lifetime Value (LTV) of a converted lead is **$45,000**.

## Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) by Channel
Based on the GTM plan, our initial channels are highly manual and organic, meaning hard-dollar CAC is $0, but we must account for labor time.
1. **LinkedIn Tear-downs:** ~4 hours of labor to generate 20 leads. Blended CAC: ~$15/lead.
2. **Slack Community Direct Outreach:** ~5 hours of active monitoring/replying to generate 15 highly qualified leads. Blended CAC: ~$25/lead.
3. **VC Portfolio Distribution:** ~2 hours of cold emailing to secure 1 VC partner who distributes to 30 companies, yielding 10 leads. Blended CAC: ~$10/lead.
**Estimated Blended CAC:** $15 - $25 per raw lead generated by the audit tool.

## The Conversion Funnel to Profitability
To understand profitability, we model the funnel from a completed audit to a closed contract.

- **Step 1: Audit Completed to Consultation Booked.** The audit report highlights $10k in savings. The CTA is "Let Credex automatically enforce these savings and manage your cards." We estimate a **10% conversion rate** from completing the audit to booking a call, as the intent is high.
- **Step 2: Consultation to Credit Purchase/Contract.** The sales team speaks to the CFO. Given the trust established by the free audit, we estimate a **25% close rate**.

**Math to acquire 1 Customer:**
- 1 Customer = 4 Consultations (25% close rate)
- 4 Consultations = 40 Audits completed (10% booking rate)
- Cost to acquire 40 Audit leads (at $20 Blended CAC) = **$800**

**Profitability:**
We spend $800 in time/labor to acquire a customer worth $15,000 in Year 1 ARR. This represents an exceptional LTV:CAC ratio of over 50:1, making the audit tool an incredibly profitable growth engine.

## Path to $1M ARR in 18 Months
To drive $1,000,000 in new ARR directly from this tool within 18 months, what must be true?
At $15,000 ARR per customer, we need **67 closed deals**.

Working backwards through our funnel:
- 67 Closed Deals requires 268 Consultations.
- 268 Consultations requires **2,680 Completed Audits**.
- Over 18 months, that equates to roughly **150 audits per month**, or 5 per day.

To achieve 150 audits per month, the manual GTM motions will not scale. We would need to transition from organic outreach to:
1. **Automated VC/Accelerator partnerships:** Embedding the audit into the onboarding checklists for Y Combinator or Techstars companies.
2. **Product-Led Growth (PLG):** The `shareId` link must act as a viral loop, where an engineering manager shares the report with their CFO, who then shares the tool with a peer at another company.
3. **Paid Social:** Once the organic funnel converts predictably at 2.5% (Audit -> Close), we can deploy paid LinkedIn ads targeting "Director of Finance", knowing we can spend up to $3,000 per acquired customer and still maintain a healthy 5:1 LTV:CAC ratio.
