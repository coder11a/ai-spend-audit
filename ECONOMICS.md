# Economics & Business Model

## Current MVP State
The AI Spend Audit is currently a **Lead Generation Magnet**. It is entirely free to use and does not gate the results behind a paywall.

## Value Exchange
- **User gets**: Immediate, actionable advice to cut costs and an aesthetic, shareable report.
- **We get**: Highly qualified leads (emails + company size + current AI stack) for a potential B2B SaaS management platform, or consulting services.

## Future Monetization Paths
1. **Spend Management Platform**: A $49/mo SaaS that actively monitors OpenAI/Anthropic API usage and alerts teams when they are pacing over budget.
2. **Enterprise Procurement**: Charging a percentage (e.g., 10%) of the total savings we negotiate or identify for large enterprises.
3. **Affiliate Revenue**: Recommending alternative tools (e.g., switching from an expensive enterprise plan to a cheaper alternative) using affiliate links.
