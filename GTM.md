# Go-To-Market Plan (GTM)

## The Target User
Our exact target user is a **Fractional CFO or Director of Finance** at a Series A or Series B software startup (50–150 employees). These individuals are hired to extend runway and transition the company from chaotic early-stage spending to disciplined growth. They are not deeply technical, but they are suddenly responsible for auditing massive, ambiguous credit card line items for "OpenAI", "Anthropic", and "GitHub".

## The Trigger Moment
They need this tool right before board meetings, during annual budget reviews, or immediately after a new funding round when they are tasked with "cleaning up the books." They are actively Googling queries like:
- "How to track OpenAI API spend across team"
- "GitHub Copilot Business vs Enterprise worth it"
- "Average AI spend per engineer benchmark"

## Where They Hang Out
They don't hang out on Hacker News or technical Discord servers. They are active in:
- **Slack Communities:** *Off The Ledger* (by Airbase), *CFO Connect*, and *Modern Finance*.
- **Newsletters:** Reading *The Generalist* or specific finance ops newsletters like *Secret CFO*.
- **LinkedIn:** Actively participating in discussions around "runway extension" and "SaaS sprawl."

## First 100 Users in 30 Days ($0 Paid Budget)
We will bypass generic content marketing and execute a highly targeted direct-outreach and community strategy:

1. **The "Tear-down" Approach (LinkedIn):** I will perform 5 public "AI spend tear-downs" of anonymous startup data (simulated or anonymized from early beta testers). I will post these on LinkedIn, tagging prominent Fractional CFOs, highlighting how a generic 100-person startup is wasting $40k/year on redundant Copilot and Cursor seats. The call-to-action will be the free audit link.
2. **Direct Value via Slack Communities:** Inside *Off The Ledger* and *CFO Connect*, I will monitor for keywords like "SaaS spend," "budget," or "OpenAI." Instead of pitching the tool, I will reply with direct advice and offer to run their stack through our "internal pricing calculator" if they DM me. I will manually run the audit for them and send them the generated `shareId` link.
3. **The "VC Value-Add" Channel:** I will cold-email 20 associates at mid-tier VC firms with a simple proposition: "Send this link to your portfolio companies; it will save them 15% on their AI tooling bills this week." VCs love free, high-leverage tools they can share with founders to look helpful.

## The Unfair Distribution Channel
Credex’s unfair advantage is its existing relationships with startup finance teams and lenders. Credex can inject this audit tool directly into the onboarding or underwriting flow for new credit lines. "To qualify for the optimal rate, complete this 2-minute AI Spend Audit to ensure your SaaS burn is optimized." No one else has the authority to make an audit a required (or highly incentivized) step in accessing capital.

## Week-1 Traction Expectations
If this works, by the end of Week 1 we should see:
- 150 unique landing page visitors.
- 40 completed audits (26% conversion rate).
- 25 captured emails of validated startup operators.
- At least 3 users sharing their public report link back into a Slack community, creating a micro-viral loop.
