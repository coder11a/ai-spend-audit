# Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy

## Target Audience
- Startup founders, CTOs, and Engineering Managers (teams of 10-100).
- Companies experiencing "AI tool sprawl" (paying for ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor, and API usage simultaneously without central governance).

## Acquisition Channels
1. **Engineering Communities**: HackerNews, Reddit (r/SaaS, r/startups), IndieHackers. The angle: "Stop paying for 3 different LLMs per developer."
2. **Content Marketing**: "The State of AI Spend in 2026", analyzing aggregated data from our audits.
3. **LinkedIn & Twitter (X)**: Sharing anonymized "Audit Tear Downs". e.g., "How we saved a 50-person agency $15k/yr on AI tools."

## Product-Led Growth
- The "Share Report" feature generates a unique, public URL (`/report/[shareId]`). When users share their results with their team, it exposes the tool to more potential users.
