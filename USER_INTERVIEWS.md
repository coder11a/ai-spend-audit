
# Interview 1 — Varun Jain, Founder at Home Creation

## Background
Varun Jain runs Home Creation, a business focused on hardware products, modular kitchen accessories, wardrobes, and interior home solutions. His team recently started adopting AI tools like ChatGPT Plus for customer communication, product descriptions, and marketing workflows.

---

## Direct Quotes

> “I liked that the audit didn’t ask for bank details or account integrations first.”

> “We were paying for multiple AI subscriptions across sales and marketing without realizing how many were actually active.”

> “The recommendations felt practical instead of overly technical.”

> “I showed the report to my operations manager because the summary was easy to understand.”

---

## Most Surprising Insight

Varun discovered that several employees had separate AI subscriptions being reimbursed by the company even though they barely used them weekly. He originally assumed most spending came from API usage, but the unnecessary cost mostly came from duplicate subscriptions.

---

## What Changed in the Design

After this conversation:
- Added clearer recommendations for unused AI seats and duplicate subscriptions.
- Kept onboarding lightweight without forcing account integrations.
- Improved the executive summary section for non-technical business owners.
- Focused more on simplicity and speed during the audit flow.

---

## Additional Notes

Varun repeatedly mentioned that small businesses avoid tools requiring complicated setup or sensitive financial access during onboarding. This reinforced the decision to make the platform frictionless during beta testing.

# Interview 2 — Abhishek Jain, Creator of FNO Builder

## Background

Abhishek Jain created FNO Builder, a platform focused on stock market and options trading analytics. He actively uses AI tools for coding assistance, automation, debugging, and API-based workflows.

---

## Direct Quotes

> “The API recommendation section was the most useful part for me.”

> “I found one automation script making unnecessary API calls in the background.”

> “Most dashboards only show spending. This one actually suggested actions to reduce it.”

> “The UI feels modern and startup-like instead of looking like accounting software.”

---

## Most Surprising Insight

Abhishek discovered that a scheduled background task was consuming unnecessary API credits continuously. The issue was not from team members overspending, but from automation inefficiencies that nobody noticed earlier.

---

## What Changed in the Design

This conversation directly influenced several improvements:
- Added recommendations for API spending caps and monitoring.
- Improved the separation between subscription costs and API/token costs.
- Added warning logic for automation-related waste.
- Shifted product focus toward optimization suggestions instead of only analytics.

---

## Additional Notes

Abhishek cared more about fast actionable insights than detailed financial reporting. That feedback changed the product direction toward becoming an “AI spend optimization assistant” instead of just an expense dashboard.

# Interview 3 — Neha Jain, YouTube Channel Manager

## Background

Neha Jain manages AI-assisted workflows for YouTube content production, including scripts, thumbnails, captions, editing support, and content planning tools. Her team uses multiple AI subscriptions for content creation and collaboration.

---

## Direct Quotes

> “The shareable audit report was honestly my favorite feature.”

> “The AI-generated summary already sounded like something I would send to my team.”

> “We subscribed to some premium AI plans just because everyone online recommended them.”

> “The report was short enough that I actually read the whole thing.”

---

## Most Surprising Insight

Neha mentioned that many AI subscriptions were purchased because of online trends or creator recommendations rather than actual workflow requirements. Several expensive plans were barely being used despite monthly renewals.

---

## What Changed in the Design

After this interview:
- Improved readability and shareability of audit reports.
- Added clearer downgrade recommendations for expensive plans.
- Simplified technical language for non-engineering users.
- Prioritized concise summaries over large analytics dashboards.

---

## Additional Notes

Neha emphasized that creators and managers prefer simple recommendations over highly technical reports. This feedback influenced the “Top Savings Opportunities” section shown at the beginning of the audit results.
