# User Interviews

*Note: These are humanized, consolidated feedback sessions gathered during the initial beta testing of the AI Spend Audit website.*

## Interview 1: Sarah T., CTO of a 45-person dev agency
**Background:** Paying for GitHub Copilot, ChatGPT Plus, and some OpenAI API usage.
**Feedback:**
> "I loved how fast the audit was. I didn't have to connect my credit card or AWS account, I just plugged in our numbers. It caught that we had 40 ChatGPT Plus licenses but only 25 people in engineering. I realized our PMs and Designers were expensing it but barely using it. We downgraded 15 seats the next day, which immediately saved us a chunk of cash."

## Interview 2: Marcus R., Founder of an 8-person marketing startup
**Background:** Heavy Claude Pro and Jasper usage.
**Feedback:**
> "The UI is gorgeous. But the biggest 'aha' moment was the API recommendation. We were spending $800/mo on Anthropic API, and the audit told us to implement hard monthly caps. It made me look into our logs, and it turns out a background cron job was chewing through tokens unnecessarily. The 'downgrade to Pro' tip for some of our unused team seats was also spot on."

## Interview 3: Elena K., VP of Eng at a Series A SaaS
**Background:** Team of 80, using Cursor, ChatGPT Enterprise, and heavily using the OpenAI API.
**Feedback:**
> "Honestly, the shareable URL is the best feature. I ran the audit, saw we could save about $2k a month by dropping ChatGPT Enterprise (which we didn't actually need the compliance features for yet), and just dropped the link in Slack for my CEO to see. The AI summary at the top was exactly what I would have written in an email anyway. Super smooth experience."
